1Information we collect

  • Game progress: high score, coins, boosters, settings and your current game, saved on your device and backed up to the cloud so you don't lose progress.
  • Player identifiers: a random player ID created by Unity Gaming Services and, if you sign in, your Google Play Games player ID. These are used to link your saved progress to you.
  • Device and advertising data: device model, operating system, language, approximate location (country/region from IP address) and the Android advertising ID, collected by our ad partner.
  • Gameplay analytics (only with your permission): events such as games played, scores, boosters used and ads watched. You decide this in the first-launch popup and can change it any time in Settings.
  • Purchase records: which items you bought and a transaction ID. Payment details are handled by Google Play – we never see your card information.

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or precise GPS location.

2How we use it

  • To run the Game and save and restore your progress across devices.
  • To deliver items you purchase and restore them (e.g. "No Ads").
  • To show ads and reward you for watching optional videos.
  • To understand how the Game is played and improve it (only if you allow analytics).
  • To turn features on or off remotely and fix problems.

4Advertising & consent

The Game shows ads between games and optional reward videos (for example, to continue a game or earn coins). Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may use your advertising ID to show personalised ads.

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice any time from Settings → Privacy Options.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Buying "No Ads" removes ads between games. Optional reward videos stay available if you want them.

5In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive only a confirmation and transaction ID so we can deliver your items. Refunds are handled under Google Play's refund policy.

6Storage & retention

Your progress is stored on your device and on Unity's cloud servers, which may be located outside your country. We keep it for as long as you play. Analytics data is kept only as long as needed to improve the Game, and is deleted on request.

7Your choices & rights

  • Analytics: turn it off any time in Settings. Turning it off also requests deletion of analytics data already collected.
  • Ads consent: change it in Settings → Privacy Options (where available).
  • Access or delete your data: email us (see below) and we will help. Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your data.
  • Uninstalling the Game removes local data from your device.

8Children

Dash Blast is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with trusted providers. No method is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will show when it changed. Continuing to play after changes means you accept the updated policy.

11Contact us

Questions or data requests? Email tinmintstudio@gmail.com — we usually reply within a few days.
